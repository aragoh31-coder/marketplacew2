❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔐 Security & Privacy

How secure is this marketplace?

We use end-to-end encryption, Tor routing, and PGP verification to ensure maximum security and privacy for all users.

What is PGP and why do I need it?

PGP (Pretty Good Privacy) encrypts your messages and verifies your identity. It's required for secure communication with vendors and support.

How does Tor protect my privacy?

Tor routes your connection through multiple encrypted layers, making it nearly impossible to trace your activity back to your real IP address.

💰 Payments & Wallets

What cryptocurrencies do you accept?

We accept Bitcoin (BTC) and Monero (XMR) for maximum privacy and security. Monero provides additional anonymity features.

How do I fund my wallet?

Navigate to your wallet dashboard and use the provided deposit addresses for BTC or XMR. Always verify the address before sending funds.

Are there withdrawal fees?

Yes, there are network fees for cryptocurrency withdrawals. These cover blockchain transaction costs and are clearly displayed before confirmation.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawals are processed within 24-48 hours after admin approval. Network confirmation times vary by cryptocurrency.

🛒 Orders & Shopping

How do I place an order?

Browse products, add items to your cart, and complete checkout using your wallet balance. Make sure you have sufficient funds before ordering.

What if my order doesn't arrive?

Contact the vendor first through our messaging system. If unresolved, open a dispute and our admin team will mediate the issue.

Can I cancel my order?

Orders can only be cancelled before the vendor marks them as shipped. Contact the vendor immediately if you need to cancel.

How do I track my order?

Check your order history in your account dashboard. Vendors will provide tracking information when available.

👥 Vendor Information

How do I become a vendor?

Apply through the vendor application page. You'll need to provide verification documents and pay a vendor bond.

How are vendors verified?

All vendors undergo a thorough verification process including identity checks, product quality reviews, and security assessments.

What if I have issues with a vendor?

Use our dispute resolution system. Our admin team will investigate and mediate any conflicts between buyers and vendors.

🆘 Support & Help

How do I contact support?

Use our messaging system to contact support. For urgent issues, mark your message as "URGENT" in the subject line.

What are your response times?

Live chat: Immediate, Support tickets: 2-24 hours, Complex issues: 1-3 days. Emergency issues are prioritized.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled case-by-case through our dispute system. Valid reasons include non-delivery, wrong items, or quality issues.
